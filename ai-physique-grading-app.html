CutRank: an AI physique grading app

CutRank is a web app for adults who want direct feedback on visible physique development. Upload a photo and it returns an S–E grade, scores for muscle groups the photo shows, and a callout of where your physique appears to need work. It is an AI interpretation of a photo, not a body-composition measurement or a coach's assessment.

How the scan works

01 / Photo

Choose an angle

Start with a front, back, legs or arms photo. Use good light and keep the relevant area in frame. The first angle is free.

02 / Analysis

Score what is visible

The AI assesses visible muscle development and conditioning for that angle. Areas hidden by pose, crop or clothing should not receive confident scores.

03 / Result

See the gap

Get a letter grade, visible-muscle scores and direct feedback on the area that appears to need attention. Use it as one input alongside training records.

CutRank uses a published S–E grading scale. An S grade describes the top band of visible development; a C describes a developing base. These are CutRank's AI grading categories, not scientific or medical classifications.

Who it is for

Beginners can use a first scan to see a starting point. Experienced lifters can use the muscle-group breakdown to question whether their training emphasis matches what appears in the photo. The aim is to make the next training discussion more specific than “Do I look good?”

If you want to compare changes over time, take repeatable progress photos with similar lighting, distance and pose. A different camera setup can change the apparent grade without a change in your physique.

What CutRank cannot know from a photo

  • Health or strength: an image cannot diagnose a condition or measure your lifts.
  • Training history: it cannot see your program, effort, recovery, injuries or substance use.
  • Exact body composition: lighting, pose, pump, clothing and camera angle affect visual estimates.
  • Your worth: a letter grade is feedback about one image, not a judgment of the person in it.

The AI can be wrong. If a photo is too dark, blurry, cropped, explicit or includes multiple people, CutRank may refuse the scan. It is intended for adults aged 18 or over.

What happens to your photo?

Your browser resizes the image before upload. The resized photo is sent to the AI provider for this scan. CutRank says it does not store the photo or use it for training. The provider processes the image under its own terms. The result and photo preview can remain in your browser; an email helps restore account access.

Quick answers

Is CutRank a real app or just a physique calculator?
CutRank is a web app that accepts a photo and generates AI feedback. Its free calculators and fitness guides are separate resources.
Does the free scan include a body-fat estimate?
No. The free scan covers one angle with an S–E grade and visible-muscle scores. The photo-based body-fat estimate is in the paid Full Body Audit.
Can CutRank replace a coach?
No. A coach can use your history, technique, goals and context. CutRank gives an AI opinion on what a photo shows, with the limits of photo-based assessment.
