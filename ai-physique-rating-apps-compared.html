The real differences

Most of these tools are solving slightly different problems. If you want to track change over time, something built around before/after deltas fits better than a single-snapshot grader. If you want to compare against other people, a leaderboard-style app does that directly. If you just want a fast, honest read with no wasted motion — grade, weak point, done — that's the gap CutRank is built for.

Nobody in this space, CutRank included, is a substitute for an in-person coach or a real body-composition scan (DEXA, etc.) — every one of these is working from photos, and photo quality, lighting, and pose all move the result.
